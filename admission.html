Admissions

How to apply

  1. Book a school tour or orientation session.
  2. Submit the application form with your child’s details.
  3. Meet with the admissions team for a short welcome conversation.
  4. Complete enrollment and receive your orientation schedule.

We welcome children ages 1–6 and provide a thoughtful onboarding process to help families feel comfortable from day one.
